Launchpad uploader

The private posting tool behind the Blast of Facts and I Wonder Why YouTube channels.

Both channels make short, true science videos for kids ages 6–10, using public NASA pictures, footage and sounds, with a kid-friendly explanation, captions and credits: Blast of Facts about space, I Wonder Why about our planet. Every video is checked and approved by hand before it's posted.

Launchpad uploader runs on the channel owner's own computer. A few times a day it uploads one approved video to the channel it was made for, marked "Made for Kids", and adds it to that channel's playlist. It uses YouTube API Services, has a single user (the owner of both channels), and is not offered to the public.

Neither channel is affiliated with or endorsed by NASA. Narration voice is AI-generated.
