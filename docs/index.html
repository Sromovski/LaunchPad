Launchpad uploader

The private posting tool behind the Blast of Facts YouTube channel.

Blast of Facts makes short, true science videos for kids ages 6–10, using public NASA pictures, footage and sounds, with a kid-friendly explanation, captions and credits. Every video is checked and approved by hand before it's posted.

Launchpad uploader runs on the channel owner's own computer. Once a day it uploads one approved video to the Blast of Facts channel, marked "Made for Kids", and adds it to the channel's playlist. It uses YouTube API Services, has a single user (the channel owner), and is not offered to the public.
