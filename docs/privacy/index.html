Privacy Policy

Launchpad uploader, used by the Blast of Facts and I Wonder Why YouTube channels. Effective 7 October 2026 (first version 27 September 2026). Home · Terms of Service

What this app is

Launchpad uploader is a private tool that runs on the channel owner's own computer. It uploads short science videos for kids, which the owner has already reviewed and approved, to the owner's own YouTube channels, Blast of Facts and I Wonder Why (each video to the channel it was made for), and adds them to that channel's playlists. It has exactly one user: the owner of both channels. It is not offered to anyone else, and nobody else can sign in to it.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What information the app uses

  • Your Google sign-in token. When the channel owner signs in, Google gives the app a token that lets it act for the owner's channel. The token is stored only in a file on the owner's own computer. It is never sent anywhere except to Google.
  • Your channel's ID and name. The app reads these to check it is posting to the intended channel (Blast of Facts or I Wonder Why) and not another one.
  • Your channel's playlists. The app looks up (and, if missing, creates) the channel's topic playlists, such as "Mars Facts for Kids" or "Ocean Facts for Kids", and adds new videos to them.
  • The videos it uploads, with their title, description and tags. These are created by the channel owner and are public on YouTube once posted.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, store or read any information about viewers, including children who watch the channel. It never reads comments, subscriber lists, watch history, analytics or messages.
  • It does not use cookies, tracking, advertising or analytics of any kind.
  • It does not sell, share or give any data to anyone. The only service it talks to about YouTube is Google.
  • It does not access any YouTube account other than the owner's Blast of Facts and I Wonder Why channels.

How long data is kept

The sign-in token stays on the owner's computer until the owner deletes it or revokes access. A local record of each upload (video ID, link, date) is kept on the same computer so the same video is never posted twice. Nothing is stored on any server run by this app, because there is none.

How to remove access

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account's security settings: myaccount.google.com/permissions → "Launchpad uploader" → Remove access. Deleting the token file on the owner's computer also stops the app from using the account. After revocation, the app can no longer upload or read anything.

Children's privacy

Both channels make videos for children and mark every video "Made for Kids" on YouTube. This app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
